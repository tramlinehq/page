Tramline balances automating tedious tasks, adding integrations for broad coverage, and providing visibility to everyone responsible for making a successful release.

## Your app in the store at a glance

Automatically retrieves and updates current app status on App Store or Play Store continuously, independent of active release cycles.

![App status](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6644e5e860f656524d6f24a5/6653684cf5f3fb273c99b33b_645cf9e001c00b558681352b_1st.png)

## Keep your entire team informed on Slack

Proactive notifications about release milestones: new release kickoffs and branch cuts, staged rollouts to percentages, full user rollouts.

![Slack notifications](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6644e5e860f656524d6f24a5/6653684c5504f1c899e65fb4_645cfac7b7db256fd86cc795_2nd.jpeg)

![More notifications](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6644e5e860f656524d6f24a5/6653684ca1e885762c0661a2_645cfae1ec1fdd09c996f904_3rd.jpeg)

![Rollout notification](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6644e5e860f656524d6f24a5/6653684cfac1df87b334985c_645cfaf0c3ed947ba3486872_4th.jpeg)

## Find the pesky build that one user is complaining about

All Builds page shows comprehensive build history searchable by number or name, with release cycles, CI workflows, distribution channels, and download links.

![All Builds](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6644e5e860f656524d6f24a5/6653684c954fcf4f02f7e874_645cfb18bee0f96cf0f9bca6_5th.gif)

## Who did what and when

Detailed event logs track all release activities categorized as neutral notices, successful operations, or notable failures.

![Event logs](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6644e5e860f656524d6f24a5/6653684c8cd521a8408ac41a_645cfb280c6ed958ad22a791_6th.png)
