Tramline sponsored Droidcon San Francisco 2024. This was a contrast from 2023, when we attended as non-sponsors. Back then, we had to approach people and pique their interest from scratch. This time, we wanted to understand what sponsorship could do for sales. The Droidcon team — Emiliano and Elissa — provided great support throughout.

## Intention

The key difference as sponsors was that attendees approached our booth already knowing Tramline was there to sell. They arrived with the intention to listen. Most engaged with product demos at the booth. Our team refined the pitch through repeated interactions over the course of the event.

We learned from Arnold at Screenshotbot, who prefers giving a high-level overview at the booth and then scheduling post-conference demos for deeper conversations.

## Making friends

We connected with other sponsors during sessions:

- **Gitar team** — we discussed mobile DevOps and the role of AI in the space.
- **Arnold (Screenshotbot)** — shared insights on selling and growing products as a solo developer.
- **Josh (Emerge Tools)** — gave us feedback on positioning and pricing, and talked about content-driven sales.
- **Sentry** — we're existing customers, and their sales engineers shared their go-to-market perspectives.

## Outro

Sponsoring conferences supports developer communities directly. For products with traction, it provides significant visibility in a short window. It's an effective use of both time and money.
